1. User & Admin Management
Dashboards: Separate, fully functional dashboards for Users and the Admin.

Registration: Direct user registration option must be available on the Home Page.

ID Activation & Validity Logic: A 'Customer ID' will have a strict validity of 30 days. After 30 days, the ID becomes inactive. No income or commission will be credited to an inactive ID.

Reactivation Logic: To reactivate/renew the ID, the user must purchase products worth 100 Points (PV). This can be a one-time purchase or accumulated through retail purchases throughout the month (reaching 100 PV automatically activates the ID).

PV Adjustment: Users can activate their IDs using only PV (without initially buying products) and later adjust the PV amount when they actually purchase physical products.

2. Hierarchy & Tree System
Tree View: No graphical or visual family tree is required on the dashboard.

Generation Display: Generations (from 1st to 5th level) should be displayed entirely in a simple Tabular format.

3. Packages & Multi-Package Logic
The system will have 3 types of packages. A single User ID can purchase multiple packages. If one package expires or is canceled, only the benefits of that specific package will stop; the others will remain active.

Customer Package: 1000 PV (1 Point = 1 BDT).

Shareholder Package: 5000 SP (1 SP = 1 BDT).

Gold Package: 5000 GP (1 GP = 1 BDT). The dashboard header must display a live countdown timer (365 days, hours, minutes, seconds) showing the validity of the Gold Package.

4. Commission & Distribution Logic (Critical Core Logic)
Commission distribution varies strictly based on the package type:

Customer Package Sale: When this package is sold, 1% of the PV will be distributed instantly up to 5 levels (Generations) and this specific amount will be credited to everyone's 'Hajj Fund'. Additionally, a standard Generation Bonus of 1% of the PV will be instantly distributed up to 5 generations.

Shareholder Package Sale: When sold, the referrer instantly receives a 2.5% commission of the SP, which is credited directly to their 'Current Balance'.

Gold Package Sale:

Referrer Income: The referrer gets a 1.8% commission. However, this is NOT instant. It must be divided by 365 and credited to the referrer's 'Current Balance' in equal daily installments as long as the buyer's package is active.

Buyer Income/Due: For the buyer, 36% of 1,00,000 GP will be divided by 365. The daily calculated amount will be credited to the buyer's 'Due Account'.

Cancellation Logic: If the buyer wishes to cancel or close their Gold Package, they must pay the total accumulated amount in their 'Due Account' back to the Admin. As soon as the package is canceled, the referrer's daily income from this package will also stop immediately.

Note: The Shareholder and Gold packages ONLY generate referrer income. All other generation bonuses and club commissions are calculated and distributed solely based on PV (Customer Packages / Product sales).

5. Clubs, Funds & Target Logic
All club names will be visible on everyone's dashboard, but income will only be credited based on specific conditions:

Fund Distribution Rates (Based on PV): Shareholder Club (10%), Daily Club (20%), Hajj Club (10%), Salary Club (3%), Reward Point (7%), Monthly Prize Point (5%), Hajj Lottery Club (5%), Refer Bonus (5% - distributed as 1% up to 5 levels into the Hajj Fund).

Shareholder Club Income: Income from this specific club is strictly reserved for users who have purchased the Shareholder Package.

Salary Club Target: If a user successfully refers 15 direct IDs (each with 1000 PV), they will automatically become a member of the 'Salary Club' and qualify for its benefits.

6. Wallet, Transfer & Withdrawal
Balance Display: The dashboard must separately display: Refer Balance, Current Balance, Total Income, Daily Club Bonus, Salary Club, Hajj Fund, and Shareholder Club.

Withdrawal Methods: Users can withdraw funds via Bank, bKash, Nagad, and Rocket.

Withdrawal Charge & Process: A strict 5% charge applies to all withdrawals. When a user requests a withdrawal, it deducts from their account and appears in the Admin's withdrawal request panel.

Fund Transfer: The system must support User ID to User ID fund/balance transfers.

7. Admin Capabilities & Security
The Admin Panel must have full authority and control over the following:

User Management: Admin can view any user's ID, view or change their passwords, and Lock/Unlock any account.

Transaction Control: Admin must have the capability to manually send Prizes or Balance to any user's ID.

Dealer Commission: Admin needs a separate feature to allocate a 5% commission on products specifically for Dealers.

Comprehensive Reporting: The system must generate detailed reports for everything: Incomes, Withdrawals, Sales, Package Activations, and Fund Distributions.
